Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get my college added?
Email us at college-requests@mycollegetix.com with your school name and student email domain. We'll review and typically add new schools within a week!
Is MyCollegeTix really free?
Yes! We don't charge any fees. Students handle transactions directly with each other. We just provide the platform for verified students to connect.
What if I have an issue with another user?
Contact our support team immediately. While we don't handle transactions, we take safety seriously and can investigate user behavior and remove bad actors.
How do you verify students?
We verify through official .edu email addresses and additional verification steps. Only current students at supported schools can join.
Can I suggest new features?
Absolutely! We're always looking to improve. Send your suggestions to hello@mycollegetix.com - we read every message.
Do you plan to expand beyond college sports?
We're focused on sports tickets for now, but we're always evaluating what would be most helpful for the student community. Let us know what you think!
